# Decision Quality vs Outcome Quality

Good decisions can lose. Bad decisions can win.

Review the process using what was knowable at the time.

## THEN

Record:
- information available;
- uncertainty visible;
- assumptions;
- alternatives;
- downside considered;
- limits;
- reasons for acting;
- update triggers.

## NOW

Record:
- outcome;
- new evidence;
- assumptions confirmed or falsified;
- unexpected consequences;
- lessons.

Do not allow hindsight to rewrite THEN using information learned NOW.

## Update

Ask:
- Was the decision process sound?
- Was position size appropriate?
- Was downside understood?
- Were the odds improvable?
- Was optionality preserved where useful?
- Did the upside justify the exposure?
- What rule, threshold, or assumption should change next time?

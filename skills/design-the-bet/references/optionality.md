# Optionality

Optionality is the value of retaining useful future choices when uncertainty remains.

## Ways to preserve options

- stage a commitment;
- pilot before scaling;
- choose modular solutions;
- negotiate exit rights;
- maintain reserves;
- avoid unnecessary lock-in;
- delay irreversible choices when waiting has informational value;
- create multiple paths to the same objective.

## Optionality has a cost

Keeping options open can consume money, attention, time, or opportunity. Do not maximize optionality blindly.

Ask:

- Which future choices are actually valuable?
- What does preserving them cost?
- When does waiting reduce rather than increase value?
- What information is likely to arrive?
- At what point should exploration become commitment?

## Reversibility

When two options have similar expected value under high uncertainty, reversibility can be a meaningful differentiator.

Irreversible decisions deserve more scrutiny before commitment.

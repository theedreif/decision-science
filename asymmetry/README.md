# Asymmetry

> **Heads I win a lot. Tails I lose a little.**

Asymmetry is about the shape of the payoff, not simply the probability of success.

A useful decision can have bounded downside and meaningful upside even when the outcome remains uncertain.

## Questions

- What is the maximum credible downside?
- Is the downside survivable?
- Is the decision reversible?
- What happens if the bet works unusually well?
- Can the same favorable bet be repeated?
- Does one failure remove future opportunities?

The objective is not to avoid loss. It is to avoid losses that remove you from the game.
